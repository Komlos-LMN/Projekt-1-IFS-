ABOUT FROSTFYRE

Frostfyre Freestyle is a team built around creativity, individuality and the passion for ice freestyle.

Our goal is to bring a different perspective to ice skating, combining unique styles, technical skills and personal expression.

We believe that freestyle is more than just tricks. It's about developing your own style and constantly pushing your limits.
